A Treatise Upon Ciphers, Lately Discovered

Old & Bleak

being  an Account of Sundry Messages  intercepted upon  the Wire,  rendered  unto the Reader by means of an Antient & Bleak Apparatus.

✦
fig. I. the cipher wheel,
slowly turning.

The apparatus, dear reader, is at present restored & attentive. Three dispatches may be solicited from its brass and bakelite innards — the first, a publick key set in lead; the second, a sealed missive; the third, a verification of one's own workings. Proceed with the decorum befitting a message older than the wires that carry it.
